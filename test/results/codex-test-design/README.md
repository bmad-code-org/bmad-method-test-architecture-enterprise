## Stable scored structure

Issue #424 recorded two unstable cases because the workflow let the model choose how many scored risks, how many coverage rows, and which levels to write. The retained plans showed the variation in exactly those fields: seven and five seeded risks, three and two clean risks, and coverage rows that ranged from ten to twenty with different level multisets. No threshold, fixture oracle or matcher changed.

The workflow now derives one scored risk per source gap statement (one residual risk per story when the epic states no gap), gives each scored risk exactly one risk-linked coverage row at the level where its failure first becomes observable, and requires well-formed risk tables whose descriptions open with the source's own key words. An earlier trial of the same guidance, before the description and table-shape rules, produced a seeded plan that described the overwrite as "destroys earlier lines" (a grounding miss) and a clean plan with a malformed delimiter row. Two repetitions in that trial were voided because the working tree changed during the run; they are not retained.
